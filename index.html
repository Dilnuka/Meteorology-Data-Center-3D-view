How to explore

Orbit (default)

  • Desktop: Left-drag rotate · Scroll zoom · Right-drag pan
  • Phone/tablet: One finger rotate · Pinch zoom/pan
  • Reset view: circular arrow in the top bar

Inspect assets

  • Click or tap racks, PAC units, UPS, fire systems, or the floor
  • Or open Menu → Jump to a system for a list
  • Turn on X-Ray Plenum to see under-floor trays

Walk inside

  • Press Walk in the top bar (or Menu)
  • Desktop: WASD / arrows move · mouse looks · Esc exits · click inspects
  • Mobile: drag screen to look · use the left stick to move · Exit Walk

Layers & demos

  • Labels — floating name pins
  • Airflow — cold-aisle containment doors
  • Thermal View — illustrative heat visualization (not live CFD)
  • Gas drill — visual simulation only
